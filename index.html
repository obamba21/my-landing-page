How it works

Three habits. Eight minutes. Real results.

Morning check-in

A 2-minute pulse on mood, energy and sleep. Cura builds a picture of your patterns over time and flags early signs of overload.

Micro-session

A short, targeted exercise (breathing, reframing, body scan) chosen by the app based on what you need that day, not a generic playlist.

Weekly insight

Every Sunday, a private report: your stress peaks, sleep trends, and one concrete focus for the week ahead.

Built for the environment you're actually in.

Most wellness apps are built for people with time and low baseline stress. Cura was designed specifically for high-pressure academic environments where performance pressure is real, schedules are brutal, and showing vulnerability is hard.

We worked with psychologists at École Normale Supérieure and student wellbeing researchers to build protocols that fit between classes, not instead of them.
